# Ready to play

1. Double-click **Start Chat Chase.command** on this Mac. Keep that terminal window open.
2. In the browser, press **Start a run**.
3. Hold **→** to run, tap **↑** to jump, hold **↓** to duck. Press **Space** to pause.
4. Demo mode is on at the launcher's address. Use “Join race”, “Drop wall”, “Drop bar” and “Send shield” to try chat effects.
5. To use your body, enable the webcam, allow access and stand still with your full body visible for three seconds.

You can also open http://localhost:5173/?demo=1 while the local server is running.

The public source is https://github.com/Pokrt/chat-chase. README.md contains Twitch setup and OBS instructions. demo.mp4 is a short keyboard gameplay preview. DEMO.md contains the final video plan.

Your Twitch application ID and sign-in are still needed for a real channel. Physical camera accuracy and frame rate need testing on your setup; committed landmark fixtures are synthetic. The YouTube upload is not published because no YouTube account connection is available.
